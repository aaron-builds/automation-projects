# Business Info — Vertex Labs

## Pitch
I help independent recruitment agencies keep new client leads coming in automatically, so business development doesn't stop the moment you get busy delivering.

## Products
1. **CH Trigger Engine (lead-intelligence engine)** — ingests newly incorporated UK companies from Companies House, enriches with director data, scores leads, delivers a ranked outreach-ready export.
2. **LLM enquiry-triage tool** — reads inbound client/candidate enquiries, classifies intent, and drafts a first response automatically, cutting manual triage time.

Both are shipped and live, not prototypes. Automated cold outreach and follow-up sequences are currently running on a schedule against independent UK recruitment agencies.

## Business Model
- **Setup fee**: £400–£1,500 one-off, depending on scope, to build and deliver the initial system.
- **Monthly retainer**: £400–£600/month for ongoing delivery, monitoring, and new leads each week.
- **Upsells** (once trust is established, priced as their own setup + smaller retainer add-on):
  - Candidate sourcing automation
  - CV triage
  - Compliance automations
