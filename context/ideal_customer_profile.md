# Ideal Customer Profile — Vertex Labs

## Fit Criteria
- **Size**: 1–15 staff. Owner or director is the decision-maker — no procurement layer, so outreach reaches the actual buyer directly.
- **Type**: Independent agencies (not part of a large national chain or franchise). Smaller agencies feel the sourcing/BD pain hardest — no dedicated BD team or research function.
- **Sectors**: Kept broad — tech, construction, healthcare, and similar niches where hiring signals are frequent and public. Avoid ultra-niche executive search firms.
- **Geography**: UK-wide, across the 40 cities the prospect finder currently covers — not London-only.

## Top Buying Signal
An agency actively posting its own job openings for business development, sales, or resourcing/researcher roles. This is the strongest available signal — it means they already recognize BD/sourcing capacity as a bottleneck and are trying to solve it by hiring, which is the exact problem Vertex Labs solves without the overhead of a new salary.

**Secondary signal**: An agency trading a while (active site/LinkedIn, real placements visible) but with thin or stale content/BD activity — a sign the desk is busy delivering and BD has gone quiet, matching the core pitch.

## Disqualifiers — Skip Even If Size/Sector Fits
- **Too new/unproven**: Founded within the last 6–12 months, no visible placements or activity history — not enough track record, cash flow usually too tight for a retainer.
- **Tool overlap already solved**: Site/LinkedIn shows a visible BD/sourcing automation stack already in use (heavy Sales Navigator activity, a named CRM automation vendor mentioned publicly) — pain already addressed elsewhere.
- **Executive search / retained-only firms**: Low volume, relationship-driven, sells on reputation not speed — automation isn't the lever that grows their business.
- **Dormant or dying agency**: No new content, no active job postings, no LinkedIn activity in 6+ months — signals the business may be winding down, not just quiet on BD.
- **Can't realistically afford it**: True one-person operation with no visible client roster or billings — retainer would be a genuine financial strain rather than an easy yes. Rare given the 1–15 staff floor, but worth checking.
