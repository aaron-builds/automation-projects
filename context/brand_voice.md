# Brand Voice — Vertex Labs

## Adjectives
Direct, plain-spoken, credible, grounded, quietly confident — a senior peer talking, not a salesperson performing.

## Avoid
- Hype / growth-hacker language: "revolutionary," "game-changing," "cutting-edge," "supercharge," "unlock," "leverage" (as a verb), "synergy," "disrupt"
- AI-sounding filler: "I hope this message finds you well," "I wanted to reach out," "in today's fast-paced world," "I'm excited to share," "let's connect"
- Overly formal or overly casual register — sits in the middle, like a competent person explaining something clearly
- Stacked exclamation marks or forced enthusiasm
- Em dashes, en dashes, and contractions — matches existing outreach rules exactly
- American spelling — British English throughout

## Underlying Principle
Say what needs saying in the first sentence, then back it with proof, not slogans. Clarity signals intelligence; needlessly complex language makes you sound less credible, not more. Nobody buys "AI-powered" — they buy the outcome (e.g. "cuts enquiry-handling time by X hours a week").

## Proof & Credibility (pre-revenue, zero clients closed)
Never claim client results that don't exist. Lean on the built system itself — it's real, running, and demonstrable, which is stronger proof than an invented testimonial.

- **Live demonstration over description** — offer to run the engine on the prospect's own market and show the actual leads it finds, rather than describing capabilities abstractly
- **Specificity as proof** — real numbers about the system (cities covered, how scoring works, that it runs automatically on a schedule) rather than vague claims like "powerful" or "cutting-edge"
- **Founder background, briefly** — self-taught, built and shipped two production systems, actively dogfooding it to run own outreach
- **Never fabricate** — no fake testimonials, no invented statistics, no implied client results

## Fixed CTA
Always the same low-friction ask: offer to run the system live on the prospect's own market and send the actual output, at no cost, no commitment. Never ask for a meeting on first contact — the free sample is the CTA.
